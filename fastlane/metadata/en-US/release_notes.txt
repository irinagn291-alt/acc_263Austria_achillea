Version 1.0 is the first release of Achillea.

Type names on the list, set Chance or turn on Even chance, Lock names, then Draw to choose one name by chance and save it in History. After a pick, use New list to continue with the same names. History groups filed picks by day and offers Undo last pick.

Includes four-page onboarding (Skip or Begin), Settings with How Draw picks, Contact, How Draw works, Re-run onboarding, and Reset all data. Names, locks, and History stay on this device. Portrait only. Dark appearance. Minimum iOS 17.0.
